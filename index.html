Who We Are

About Vijay Vidhyanikethan High School

Building the foundation for every child's future since 1996

1996 Est.

Vijay Vidhyanikethan High School has been a cornerstone of quality education in Thandriyal, Telangana since 1996. We offer Classes 1 through 10 under the State Board curriculum, with a strong focus on academic excellence, discipline and the overall development of every student.

Our school provides a safe, nurturing and student-focused environment where children are encouraged to learn, grow and realise their full potential. We believe that education must go beyond textbooks — instilling values, confidence and a love of learning in every child.

With modern learning facilities, committed guidance and a supportive school community, we strive to prepare students for the challenges of tomorrow while grounding them in the values of today.
